About

A family game

I made this game for my family. It was made using ChatGPT in a single evening, probably in less than an hour - it really didn't take many prompts.

It is just for my family to play and is not in any way supposed to be a commercial project. Actually, I didn't even want to put it online, but trying to locally host it on my Mum's iPad was a pain in the backside since iPads don't like reading index files...
